Sugarbox privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Sugarbox is a single-player game that works offline. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. Sugarbox does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, stickers, settings) is saved only on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game. If you use the save code in Settings to move your progress, the code is copied to your clipboard and goes wherever you paste it; we never receive it.

Permissions

The game asks only for permission to vibrate, for tap feedback. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Children and parents

Sugarbox is made to be suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children under 13 (or the age set in your country). Specifically, the game:

  • has no ads and no third-party code that collects data,
  • has no accounts, chat, sharing or way to contact other players,
  • has no in-app purchases,
  • does not use device identifiers or location,
  • has no links that leave the game.

Parents or guardians with any question can write to the contact address below. All progress is on the device and can be removed by uninstalling the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
